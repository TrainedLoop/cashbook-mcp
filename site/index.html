Ask your assistant about your money. It can only read it.

An MCP server you run yourself. It syncs your bank accounts, cards, investments and loans from Pluggy (through the free MeuPluggy connector, Brazilian Open Finance) into an encrypted database on your own disk, and lets Claude answer questions about spending, bills, recurring charges, net worth and the cash you'll have next month. If you run a company, your personal and business money are kept apart.

Set it up GitHub

  • Read-only toward your banks

    The server never changes anything at Pluggy. The only local write is the assistant fixing a transaction's classification after asking you: journaled, capped and reversible. The SQL tool accepts a single SELECT in a locked-down sandbox.

  • Your data, your disk

    One DuckDB file, encrypted, with the key kept outside the data volume. History keeps growing past what Pluggy provides.

  • Safe against hostile text

    Anyone can send you a PIX with a message. Transaction text is cleaned, capped and flagged, and the assistant is told it is data.

What the assistant can do

ToolPurpose
finance_overviewStart here: balances, cards, upcoming bills, month to date, business block, net worth, forecast, connection problems
search_transactions, get_transactionsFiltered, paginated search; full rows by id
summarize_transactions, cash_flowAggregations by scope (personal, business, consolidated) and by one or two dimensions
net_worth, cash_forecastNet worth over time; projected cash balance for the next 30 to 90 days
find_recurring, recurring_changesRecurring charges; price increases, new, stopped and duplicate charges
list_changesWhat changed since the last run, so scheduled routines resume where they stopped
review_classification, reclassify_transactions, undo_reclassificationTransactions the server is unsure about; fix their kind or category, undo a batch
list_bills, list_investments, list_loansCards, investments and loans
describe_schema, query_sqlThe schema, and SELECT-only SQL over it

Ready-made prompts: monthly_review, weekly_review, upcoming_bills, subscriptions_audit, review_classifications. The full list of tools is in the README.

One owner per instance: only Google logins in ALLOWED_EMAILS get in. An owner page at /admin connects banks with MeuPluggy, sets whether each connection is personal or business and what each account is for, and works on a phone.
